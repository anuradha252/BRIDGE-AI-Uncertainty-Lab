# BRIDGE-AI Uncertainty Lab

An interactive, gamified educational simulation designed to help students and researchers understand uncertainty in clinical AI and its role in clinical decision-making.

The simulation accompanies **BRIDGE-AI (Bridging Clinical and AI Uncertainty for Decision Guidance)**, a conceptual framework for quantifying, contextualizing, communicating, and reasoning with uncertainty.

## Overview

Using an illustrative acute heart failure (AHF) ICU scenario, participants explore five clinical decision points involving missing data, model uncertainty, conflicting evidence, and abstention.

The simulation demonstrates:

- **Five uncertainty constructs:** Source, Nature, Impact, Resolvability, and Response.
- **Four framework functions:** Quantification, Contextualization, Communication, and Reasoning.
- **Bidirectional clinician–AI reasoning:** How clinical inputs influence AI outputs and how AI uncertainty informs clinical decisions.
- **Uncertainty continuity:** How uncertainty can be preserved or obscured across AI pipeline components.

## Features

- Five interactive clinical scenarios
- Clinician–AI interrogation and decision challenges
- Immediate feedback and gamified scoring
- Uncertainty visualization and event tracking
- Reflection questions and downloadable learning transcripts

## Disclaimer

**For educational and research demonstration purposes only.** The simulation uses fictional patient scenarios, illustrative uncertainty values, and scripted AI responses. It does not use real patient data or live predictive models and is not intended for clinical decision-making.
